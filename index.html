How it's made?

The process begins with sugarcane waste, known as bagasse, which is left over after extracting juice from the crop.
This fibrous material is cleaned and processed into pulp, which is then molded under heat and pressure to create sturdy, biodegradable tableware such as plates, bowls, and cups.

These products serve as an eco-friendly alternative to plastic disposables. After use, the tableware can be composted, where it naturally decomposes into organic matter, enriching the soil without leaving harmful residues.
This creates a sustainable cycle that turns agricultural waste into useful products and returns it safely back to the environment.
